How do we do it?

With you every step of the way.

We’ll advise on which carpets would work best for you and your home.

01

Measure & plan

We measure your spaces and work out a carpet plan to give you a competitive quotation by email.

02

Secure your order

A 50% deposit secures the price and order. The remaining 50% is payable on completion.

03

Arrange fitting

Once your carpet arrives, we’ll contact you to arrange a fitting date, usually within a few weeks.

04

Fitting day

Our courteous fitters prepare your floors, lay high-grade underlay if you’re having it, and leave your home hoovered and ready.

Lifestyle flooring

Flooring that fits the way you live.

The right floor complements your lifestyle. Entertaining often or a busy family? A durable, easy-clean option may suit best. Value luxury and elegance? Perhaps wool carpet or hardwood. We can also help zone open-plan spaces, with carpet in one area and hard flooring in another, while keeping the whole space cohesive.
