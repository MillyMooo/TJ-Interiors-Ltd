Your personal mood board

Relax, browse, and see it all together.

Come and relax with a tea or coffee and browse through the samples. With so much under one roof, you can treat the showroom as your own personal mood board — choosing wallpapers, flooring and fabrics to complement each other, and getting a real feel for the finished look.

  • Fabrics, wallpapers, carpets and hard flooring side by side
  • Expert advice from Trudi and Tim
  • Free parking right outside
The workshop

Made by hand, in full view.

Our showroom also houses our workshop, where all of our hand-finished curtains and blinds are professionally made by Trudi and her team — in full view of the showroom.

The attention to detail is precise, with care taken to create a beautiful finish in everything we do.

Finding us

Look out for the TJ Interiors sign.

We’re on the A259 Bognor Road, close to the Brick Kiln Garden Centre — you may remember our building as the old India Gate or King’s Head. We’re the first turning just after the Drayton roundabout, with free parking just outside.
