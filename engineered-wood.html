Explained

What is engineered wood?

Layers of wood bonded together for better strength, stability and durability than a single solid board, with a real wood surface you can see and feel.

More stable

The layered construction resists movement, so it suits more rooms than solid wood.

Sustainably sourced

Our Kährs floors make efficient use of timber, for real wood with a lighter footprint.

Made for real life

Kids, pets, toys and daily tasks: durable finishes built to keep looking beautiful.
