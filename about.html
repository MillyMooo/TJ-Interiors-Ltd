Meet Tim

Flooring & shutters

Tim’s a carpenter by trade with years of experience in interiors. His attention to detail is second to none, and he dedicates himself to a beautiful finish on hardwood floors and shutters.

His team of professional fitters lay engineered hardwood, laminate, luxury vinyl tile, sheet vinyl and carpets. Everything is fitted perfectly, tidy and clean, so you can move your furniture straight back in.

Call Tim · 07507 904519

Meet Trudi

Curtains, blinds & soft furnishings

Trudi specialises in soft furnishings, with a passion for colours and textures that soften interiors. She’ll help you choose fabrics that complement each other and turn the room you’re imagining into reality.

With many years’ experience working with fabrics, Trudi and her team hand-make curtains, blinds, cushion covers and lampshades in our workshop, each one finished with care and real attention to detail.

Call Trudi · 07956 712408
