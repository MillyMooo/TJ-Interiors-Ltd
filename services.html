How it works

Three simple steps to a room you love.

01

Your appointment

We arrange a convenient appointment — most customers prefer a visit at home, but you’re very welcome at our showroom in Chichester. While booking, we’ll talk through your ideas so we can bring the right samples and allow enough time for your project.

  • Options, colours, schemes and budget discussed in detail
  • As many samples as you need — you’ll be spoilt for choice
  • Detailed measurements taken for your quotation
  • Itemised quote and estimated fitting date emailed within 48 hours

Samples can be posted, taking 3–10 days depending on the supplier.

02

Placing your order

When you’re ready, a 50% deposit confirms your order. We then order your materials and begin work on your project, and you’ll receive a confirmed delivery and/or fitting date.

  • 50% deposit to secure your price and order
  • Balance due on the day of supply or fitting
  • Orders can be amended once in progress — any handling or labour costs incurred are invoiced and a revised order raised
03

Fitting day — the fun part

Seeing your design come to life. We use our own tools and protect your home where necessary. We’re fully insured and take full responsibility for our staff.

  • Our own experienced, courteous fitting team
  • Your home protected and left clean and tidy
  • Final payment by BACS, card, cash or cheque

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you visit customers at home?

Yes — most customers prefer a home visit, and we’ll bring a selection of samples based on what we discussed when booking. You’re also welcome to visit our showroom in Chichester.

How quickly will I receive my quotation?

Your itemised quotation, along with an estimated fitting date, is emailed to you within 48 hours of your appointment.

What deposit do you require?

A 50% deposit confirms your order and secures the price. The balance is due on the day of supply or fitting.

Can I change my order once it’s placed?

You can amend an order once in progress, but any handling or labour costs already incurred will be invoiced and a revised order raised.

How can I pay?

Final payment can be made by BACS transfer, card, cash or cheque.

Are your fitters insured?

Yes. We’re fully insured, use our own tools, protect your home where necessary and take full responsibility for our staff.
