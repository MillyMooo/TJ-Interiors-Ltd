Plantation shutters

Who doesn’t love shutters? Style, light control and privacy, supplied and fitted to virtually any shape or size of window.

Get a free quoteCall Tim

Beautiful, practical, and built to last.

Plantation shutters add style and beauty to any design scheme, with great control of light and privacy, plus the added benefit of insulating against the cold of winter and the heat of summer.

  • Lightweight, sustainably sourced hardwood, not MDF
  • Many paint colours and finishes to suit any interior
  • British-made faux wood range for bathrooms, wet rooms and kitchens

Find the right shutter for you.

Full height

Floor-to-ceiling panels for a clean, classic look and complete control.

Café style

Covering the lower half of the window for privacy while keeping the light.

Tier on tier

Top and bottom panels that open independently for total flexibility.

Tracked

Sliding or folding panels on a track, ideal for bi-fold and patio doors.
