Roman blinds, made in house and finished by hand.

All of our Roman blinds are made in house to order, supplied with tracking that meets all child safety regulations. A full-length continuous beaded chain makes them easy to use, thanks to the breakaway system on our tracks.

  • Hand-stitched finish, with no machine stitching on the front face
  • Blind depth planned with you so you don’t lose too much light, or fitted outside the reveal
  • Level folds or a waterfall finish
  • Relaxed Romans, London blinds and roll-up blinds too
  • Add fringe or pompom trimming. The choices are endless

Blinds questions

Are your blinds child safe?

Yes. Our Roman blinds are supplied with tracking that meets all the criteria for child safety regulations, including a breakaway system for the continuous beaded chain.

Can my blinds be motorised?

Roller blinds can be motorised and operated by remote control or a smart home system, and can even be programmed to open and close at set times.

Will a Roman blind block too much light?

We’ll plan the depth of the blind when fully open with you, and can fit it outside the window reveal to avoid losing light.
