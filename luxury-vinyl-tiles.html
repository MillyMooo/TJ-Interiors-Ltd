What is luxury vinyl?

How is LVT made?

Luxury vinyl tile (LVT) or plank (LVP) is built in layers: a wear layer on top to protect against scratches and stains, a design layer with a printed image of wood or stone, and a rigid or flexible core for stability and moisture resistance.

Which rooms is it suitable for?

Any room in the home. It’s especially popular in kitchens, bathrooms, hallways and busy family spaces thanks to its water resistance.

Is it good value?

LVT is often a cost-effective alternative to natural materials, without sacrificing style or durability.
