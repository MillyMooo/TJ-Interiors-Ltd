Why laminate

Made for the busiest rooms.

We recommend laminate for the busiest areas of the home where messes are likely. It’s easy to clean, scratch-resistant, and doesn’t hold on to mould, pests or dust, making it ideal for family homes and allergy sufferers.

  • Large range of samples to browse in our Chichester showroom
  • Love-Aqua water-resistant laminate range
  • Smooth, seamless finish that emulates real wood
  • An affordable alternative to natural materials
The secret

Four layers, fused together.

Laminate is made by fusing multiple layers using heat and pressure. The result is a smooth floor, clear of seams.

01

Wear layer

A clear protective top layer that guards against scratches and damage.

02

Design layer

A high-resolution image that gives the realistic look of wood, stone or tile.

03

Core layer

High-density fibreboard for stability and durability.

04

Backing layer

Balances the floor and helps keep moisture from seeping through.

Good to know

Laminate questions

Is laminate easy to install?

Yes. It usually comes in interlocking planks or tiles that fit together without adhesive or nails. Our professional fitters can lay it for you.

Is laminate suitable for kitchens and bathrooms?

Standard laminate can be susceptible to moisture damage, so for wetter areas we’d suggest our water-resistant Love-Aqua range, or luxury vinyl tiles or sheet vinyl.

Is it good for allergy sufferers?

Laminate doesn’t hold on to dust particles, mould or pests, making it a great choice for allergy sufferers.
